# 47b — the world edit mode, the fill, fog as voxels, dressing rules and ground cover

## What changed
- A voxel world is edited in the editor: select it, press **Edit world**. Rails: Sculpt (add, dig, smooth, flatten), Paint (materials with variants, wetness, noise mask; fog brush), Fill (fog to a level, drain, water as a water body), Splines (road, path, river), Rules (what grows under the cursor and why not). Each stroke is one `editVoxels` command, the one an agent sends, on the one undo line.
- Fog is voxels: the `gas` role (value 10), a gas material (colour, full density), drawn near the eye as a plain see-through volume in the Scene view and Play. A fill that would leak writes nothing and marks where.
- Ground cover grows by rules: a material names a dressing asset (species with ten conditions); cover is made a chunk column in the workers, never stored, re-made as you sculpt, paint or edit the dressing. Four starter plants and three dressings ship in the Basic Kit; a material has a Dressing card.
- Fields derived from voxels, one arithmetic (`ctx.voxels.surfaceAt`: height, slope, material, sky, wetness, water, gas, two distances); a role traits table every consumer asks, with a build check.
- Four of 47T's picture defects fixed: D827 (the slab band), D825 (the Profiler on a running Play), D831 (the wire grid), D832 (the bench's dig). D829 is a kit default, by design.

## Try it
`TL_STAGING_HOST=0.0.0.0 tools/staging.sh start` in `~/projects/thirdlight-p45`, open a scene with a voxel world (in a fresh project: Create → Basic Kit first), select the world and press **Edit world**; on Sculpt or Paint press **Make a grass material** and paint. Stop with `tools/staging.sh stop`, but not within a few seconds of an edit (D845).
