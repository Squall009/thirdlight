# Performance recommendations, to revisit in the performance pass after 49

The roadmap places a performance pass over the whole voxel world after 49 (`docs/roadmap.md`, "Order", item
10). This page is its starting list. An item lands here when it is **measured**, **inside the frame time today**,
and **not worth a phase's time now** (owner, 2026-11-25: "Since we are still under the current frame time add it to a
performance recommendation list that we can revisit after 49"). Nothing here is new: every number is copied from the
source named in its row (plan sections are of `docs/plan-phase-<id>.md`; a D-row named here was closed into this page in
47T.5 and is in `docs/audit-closed.md`, or is still open in `docs/audit-2026-09-22.md`). Rows 20–44 came from the defect
list's measured costs in 47T.5, several from before the voxel branch (1080p, the 60 Hz cap, the village and level
classes): their settings are in their source. Low tier, WebGPU, 1280 × 720, the 30 Hz cap, on the Iris Xe, unless the
source says otherwise. A row leaves the page when the pass fixes it, decides against it, or a phase fixes it on the
way (say so in its source).
