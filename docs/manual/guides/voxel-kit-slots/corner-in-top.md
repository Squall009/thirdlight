# Slot `corner-in-top`

> Generated by `node tools/voxel-bench/gen-slot-sheets.mjs` from the engine's case catalogue (`edges-draft` v3, role spec v1) and the kit check; do not edit by hand. [All slots](index.md) · [Make a kit and a material set](../voxel-kits.md)

An inner corner: seven solid cells of eight (a room's corner), where three inner edges meet. It closes the coves' ends. Mixed corners with five or more solid cells (a wall's foot on a wider foundation) also draw this slot.

## Facts

- **Slot id:** `corner-in-top` (the kit record's `slots` key; a mesh node named `corner-in-top_v0`, `corner-in-top_v1` … by the templates)
- **What it is:** inner corner, the open cell below it
- **Placed by:** the edge pass, one piece a lattice corner, yaw 0–3
- **Roles:** every construction role
- **Collision, navigation, light:** from the voxels, never from the piece
- **LODs:** none: pieces draw in the near ring (level 0) only; from level 1 out the edge is a plain voxel edge

## Where it sits: pivot, axes, run

The mesh is written in the case's own orientation, its origin at the **centre of its anchor cell** (the cell is −0.25 … +0.25 m on each axis). The engine turns it into place by quarter turns about up.

| | glTF / engine (+y up) | Blender (Z up) |
|---|---|---|
| Edge middle / corner point (anchor) | (0.25, -0.25, 0.25) | (0.25, -0.25, -0.25) |
| Open side (out of the solid) | (0.5774, -0.5774, 0.5774) | (0.5774, -0.5774, -0.5774) |
| Runs along | +x | +X |
| Length along the run | a corner: reach 0.125 m from the point | same |

## Envelope (what the kit check tests)

- **Must touch** (else an error: it draws beside its cell): x -0.25 … 0.25, y -0.25 … 0.25, z -0.25 … 0.25 — Blender X -0.25 … 0.25, Y -0.25 … 0.25, Z -0.25 … 0.25
- **Should stay within** (else a warning: it overlaps the neighbours' pieces): x -0.5 … 0.5, y -0.5 … 0.5, z -0.5 … 0.5 — Blender X -0.5 … 0.5, Y -0.5 … 0.5, Z -0.5 … 0.5
- Apply rotation and scale; leave the object at the template's slot origin. No faces against the voxel body or under a roof plane (hidden geometry).

## Openings (doors, windows)

Where this piece would lie inside an `opening` cell (a cove where a doorway's floor meets a jamb) it is **not placed**.

## Stagger and jitter

Every piece is moved 0.78 cm–1.56 cm **into the solid** along (-0.5774, 0.5774, -0.5774) (its open direction reversed) and twisted up to 2°, hashed per cell, scaled by the kit's `jitter` (0–4, default 1). Let the piece stand at least 1.56 cm proud of its faces at jitter 1 (6.25 cm at 4), or it sinks under them in places. Collision never sees the stagger.

## Mirror and rotation

- A slot mesh may set `mirror`: drawn mirrored across the run (left and right along x), winding and normal-map tangents flipped, so one carving serves both hands.
- `rotation` (one of the 24 cube rotations, 0–23) turns a mesh authored for another slot into this one; it never mirrors.
