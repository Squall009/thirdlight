# Slot `edge-out-top`

> Generated by `node tools/voxel-bench/gen-slot-sheets.mjs` from the engine's case catalogue (`edges-draft` v3, role spec v1) and the kit check; do not edit by hand. [All slots](index.md) · [Make a kit and a material set](../voxel-kits.md)

An outer edge: one solid cell of the four round a 0.5 m lattice edge. The piece rounds or dresses the voxel's square edge; the two faces it sits on are plain voxel faces drawn with the material's 4 × 4 m tile.

## Facts

- **Slot id:** `edge-out-top` (the kit record's `slots` key; a mesh node named `edge-out-top_v0`, `edge-out-top_v1` … by the templates)
- **What it is:** outer edge, the solid cell below it: a wall top, a stair nosing, a floor edge
- **Placed by:** the edge pass, one piece a 0.5 m lattice edge, yaw 0–3
- **Roles:** every construction role
- **Collision, navigation, light:** from the voxels, never from the piece
- **LODs:** none: pieces draw in the near ring (level 0) only; from level 1 out the edge is a plain voxel edge

- Apply rotation and scale; leave the object at the template's slot origin. No faces against the voxel body or under a roof plane (hidden geometry).

## Openings (doors, windows)

Next to an `opening` cell this slot is still drawn, **cut flush** 0.39 cm inside the anchor's faces toward the opening, so nothing stands in the doorway. Keep the part of the piece that matters on the solid side; a jamb is a vertical outer edge, a lintel a bottom edge, a sill a top edge.

## Stagger and jitter

Every piece is moved 0.78 cm–1.56 cm **into the solid** along (0, -0.7071, -0.7071) (its open direction reversed) and twisted up to 2°, hashed per cell, scaled by the kit's `jitter` (0–4, default 1). Let the piece stand at least 1.56 cm proud of its faces at jitter 1 (6.25 cm at 4), or it sinks under them in places. Collision never sees the stagger.

## Mirror and rotation

- A slot mesh may set `mirror`: drawn mirrored across the run (left and right along x), winding and normal-map tangents flipped, so one carving serves both hands.
- `rotation` (one of the 24 cube rotations, 0–23) turns a mesh authored for another slot into this one; it never mirrors.
