# Slot `roof-valley`

> Generated by `node tools/voxel-bench/gen-slot-sheets.mjs` from the engine's case catalogue (`edges-draft` v3, role spec v1) and the kit check; do not edit by hand. [All slots](index.md) · [Make a kit and a material set](../voxel-kits.md)

A roof edge, placed by the roof mesher on the edges of its sloped planes (45° and 1:2). Author it for a 45° roof; the engine pitches it onto a 1:2 roof. No piece goes round a roof plane cell otherwise.

## Facts

- **Slot id:** `roof-valley` (the kit record's `slots` key; a mesh node named `roof-valley_v0`, `roof-valley_v1` … by the templates)
- **What it is:** an inside corner line of two roof planes
- **Placed by:** the roof mesher, on the edges of roof planes
- **Roles:** roof
- **Collision, navigation, light:** from the voxels, never from the piece
- **LODs:** none: pieces draw in the near ring (level 0) only; from level 1 out the edge is a plain voxel edge

## Openings (doors, windows)

Not affected by openings.

## Stagger and jitter

Every piece is moved 0.78 cm–1.56 cm **into the solid** along (-0.4082, -0.8165, -0.4082) (its open direction reversed) and twisted up to 2°, hashed per cell, scaled by the kit's `jitter` (0–4, default 1). Let the piece stand at least 1.56 cm proud of its faces at jitter 1 (6.25 cm at 4), or it sinks under them in places. Collision never sees the stagger.

## Mirror and rotation

- A slot mesh may set `mirror`: drawn mirrored across the run (left and right along x), winding and normal-map tangents flipped, so one carving serves both hands.
- `rotation` (one of the 24 cube rotations, 0–23) turns a mesh authored for another slot into this one; it never mirrors.
- On a 1:2 roof the engine squashes the piece to half its height about its line, so both sides lie on the shallower planes; leave room for it.
- At an odd hip roof's apex the engine draws a half piece (the +x half of its run); no extra mesh.
