# Voxel kit slots

> Generated by `node tools/voxel-bench/gen-slot-sheets.mjs` from the engine's case catalogue (`edges-draft` v3, role spec v1) and the kit check; do not edit by hand.

A voxel kit fills **slots**: the 17 places where voxel faces meet (edges, corners, roof edges) and the faces round a damage hole. Every slot is optional: an empty slot draws a plain voxel edge. No piece ever goes on a flat face. How to make a kit: [Make a kit and a material set](../voxel-kits.md). What the engine stores and why: [voxel worlds](../../features/voxel-worlds.md#voxel-kits).

**Conventions for every slot** (glTF axes; Blender in brackets): metres; the mesh origin at the centre of its anchor cell (−0.25 … +0.25 m); the open side toward +z (Blender −Y, the Front view), a run along +x (+X), up +y (+Z); vertical edges run up +y and open toward +x and +z. One kit uses one texture set. No LODs (pieces draw at level 0 only); collision from the voxels.
