## Stagger and jitter

Every piece is moved 0.78 cm–1.56 cm **into the solid** along (0, 0, -1) (its open direction reversed) and twisted up to 2°, hashed per cell, scaled by the kit's `jitter` (0–4, default 1). Let the piece stand at least 1.56 cm proud of its faces at jitter 1 (6.25 cm at 4), or it sinks under them in places. Collision never sees the stagger.

The engine spreads the pieces of one face over a jittered grid within ±0.15 m of the face's centre and spins each about the face's normal: stage 2 draws 6 a face, stage 3 3 (the kit's `damage` field changes the counts).

## Mirror and rotation

- A slot mesh may set `mirror`: drawn mirrored across the run (left and right along x), winding and normal-map tangents flipped, so one carving serves both hands.
- `rotation` (one of the 24 cube rotations, 0–23) turns a mesh authored for another slot into this one; it never mirrors.
