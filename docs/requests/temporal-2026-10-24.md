# Thirdlight → Temporal: answers (2026-10-24)

The engine's answers to Temporal's requests in `docs/engine-gaps.md` (the
Temporal repo). Thirdlight never edits the Temporal repo; Temporal updates
its own table after checking.

## G11 — see-through fades walls behind the subject: fixed

Fixed in phase 39b, item 39b.6e (commits `b19cd4b6`, `fee039d5`,
`e2856b4c`, `f08433da`; the setting, manual and test `b12167fd`, `efd141fb`, `69ab53c4`). The view-depth plane test is
gone. See-through now fades only **true occluders**: per subject the engine
finds what stands in the space from the camera to the subject's body (its
capsule), widened by a padding and ending at the body. Block layer hits are
grown into whole pieces (a wall run, a pillar, a slab), and placed models
count by their bounds. A pixel fades only when it belongs to one of those
occluders. It must also be nearer the camera than the body **along its own
view ray** by the depth margin, and higher than half the subject's height
above its feet. Under a 52° follow camera, walls 0.3 m and 1 m behind the
subject and walls beside it stay solid. A wall in front, over the waist,
fades.

What to change in Temporal:
- `see_through_depth_margin_m` can go back to the default **0.25**
  (`tools/build_project.py` sets 1.0 today). A wall within half a metre in
  front thins over the waist again.
- New setting **`see_through_padding_m`** (default 0.3 m, range 0–10): how
  far the camera-to-body volume is widened when occluders are found. Raise
  it if pieces just beside the line of sight (a door frame, a pillar's edge)
  should fade too. Scripts can set it with
  `ctx.camera.setSeeThrough({padding})`, and the **Set see-through** graph
  node has the same input.
- Cost on an Iris Xe at the 60 Hz cap (two-storey interior, 4 subjects):
  frames stay at p95 ≤ 16.8 ms with no missed frames. GPU time with
  see-through on vs off went from +0.90 ms to +1.43 ms. The owner is still
  judging that.

The owner's look in Temporal is still pending: please check the interiors
with the follow camera and close G11 in your table if it holds.
